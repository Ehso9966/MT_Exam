How to Use MT Exam

Create a professional မေးခွန်းစာရွက် (exam paper) in just a few minutes.

Step 1 — Open the app

Open the MT Exam app. Everything runs in your browser — no sign-up or installation needed.

Step 2 — Set up your exam

  1. Type the file name of your paper.
  2. Add a section title (e.g. ဘာသာရပ်၊ အတန်း).
  3. Set the subject, class, time, and total marks.

Step 3 — Add questions

  • Type manually: Write each question by hand.
  • Use AI OCR: Upload a photo of questions and let AI extract them (server-side key configured in the AI settings).
  • Add math: Use the math toolbar for LaTeX formulas rendered with KaTeX.

Step 4 — Check with validation

The status bar shows a live check of marks, numbering, and question completeness. Fix any warnings before printing.

Step 5 — Print or save as PDF

Use the Print button. The A4 stylesheet includes Padauk and Pyidaungsu Myanmar fonts, so your paper prints correctly. Save drafts in your browser or export/import JSON.

⚠️ Your exam content stays in your browser — nothing is uploaded. If you use AI OCR, your API key is sent directly from the browser, so avoid using a shared or valuable key.
