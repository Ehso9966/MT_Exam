MT Exam Features

Everything you need to create a beautiful မေးခွန်းစာရွက် (exam paper) — free, in your browser.

🤖 AI OCR — Extract questions from photos

Take a photo of a handwritten or printed exam and MT Exam extracts the questions for you using the B.AI API (bring your own key). You review and confirm everything — the teacher always decides.

📚 Question types

  • MCQ (multiple choice)
  • True / False (မှန်/မှား)
  • Short answer (အတိုဖြေ)
  • Detailed answer (အသေးစိတ်ဖြေ)
  • Math / equations (သင်္ချာ)

🧮 KaTeX math

Write LaTeX formulas and they render beautifully — perfect for mathematics exam papers.

✂️ Batch crop

Split a full page photo into multiple questions with the built-in batch crop tool.

✅ Validation engine

Automatic checks on total marks, question numbering, and completeness so your paper is error-free before printing.

🖨️ Print / PDF

A4 print stylesheet with Padauk and Pyidaungsu Myanmar fonts. Print directly or save as PDF.

💾 Save & privacy

Your exam content stays in your browser — nothing is uploaded. Save drafts locally and export/import JSON.
